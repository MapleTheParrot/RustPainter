# RustPainter branding policy

The RustPainter name, logo, application icon (`RustPainterIcon.png`), and
related visual branding identify the original RustPainter project published at
<https://github.com/YeheyaMohammad01/RustPainter>.

Forks and modifications are welcome under GPL-3.0-only. You may truthfully
describe your project as “Based on RustPainter,” “Fork of RustPainter,” or
“Modified from RustPainter,” and may use the RustPainter name where necessary
for accurate attribution.

Do not present a fork, modified build, executable, website, or release as:

- the official RustPainter project or an official RustPainter release;
- maintained, supported, released, or approved by the original RustPainter
  maintainers; or
- endorsed by the original RustPainter project.

If a modified project uses RustPainter branding, it must make its unofficial or
modified status clear wherever that branding could otherwise cause users to
believe it is official. A visible project/release description and the
attribution notice required by [ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md](ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md) are
normally appropriate ways to do this. A distinct project name and icon are
recommended for public modified distributions.

This policy does not prohibit truthful attribution or lawful nominative use.
The GPL license covers copyright permissions; it does not grant trademark
rights. Nothing in this policy asserts trademark rights beyond those available
under applicable law.
